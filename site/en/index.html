How it works

Ask. Compare.
Decide.

AI finds options, PoB runs the numbers, and you choose.

  1. 01

    You ask

    “How can I improve?”, “why do I keep dying?”, or anything else about your build.

  2. 02

    AI reads the build

    It looks at the gems, items, tree, and other parts needed to answer.

  3. 03

    It suggests improvements

    It can suggest changes to gems, items, the tree, ascendancy, and configuration.

  4. 04

    PoB tests it first

    Path of Building calculates how the build would perform without changing the original.

  5. 05

    You decide

    Compare damage and defenses. The build only changes when you confirm.

Your key

Your key stays
on your computer.

You use your own AI key. It is stored only on your computer and sent only to the service you choose.

See the security details
  • Stored on your computer

    The key is hidden on screen and never included on GitHub or in the download.

  • Secure connection

    The app only talks to services through a secure connection (HTTPS).

  • No tracking

    V1 does not track how you use the app or send your chats through our own server.

  • Clean download

    The package contains no keys, settings, logs, or personal builds.

Important:

When you chat, your AI service receives the question and the parts of your build needed to answer. Choose a service you trust.
